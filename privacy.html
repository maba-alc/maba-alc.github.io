Privacy Policy

Household PDF Splitter — last updated 2 November 2026

Household PDF Splitter is a personal tool used only by its owner. It accesses the owner's own Google Drive, Google Sheets and Gmail solely to split scanned PDFs, log results, and send status emails to the owner.

  • No data is collected from, or shared with, anyone else.
  • No data is sold or used for advertising.
  • Document pages are sent to Google's Gemini API and to the owner's own Google Cloud project for processing; nothing is retained by this tool outside the owner's Google account.
